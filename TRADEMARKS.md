## What you can do without asking

- Say that your project is "based on AnswerOnce", "a fork of AnswerOnce" or "compatible with
  AnswerOnce", as long as it is clear that it is not the official project.
- Use the name and logo to refer to the official project, for example in articles, reviews,
  tutorials or talks.
- Build, run and modify AnswerOnce for your own use, keeping the name.

## What needs written permission

- Publishing a fork, a modified build or a derived product (an extension, an app, a hosted service)
  under the name AnswerOnce, a confusingly similar name, or with the parrot logo.
- Listing a build in an extension store or app store under the AnswerOnce name.
- Using the name or logo in a way that suggests endorsement by, or affiliation with, the project.

If you publish a fork, give it a different name and logo, and remove the AnswerOnce branding from
the manifest, the UI, the landing and the icons.
